Christ Community Church, Omaha

Your degree is done. Your calling is just getting started.

CCC Church Residency is a two-year, hands-on ministry program for recent grads and early career adults. Serve on a church staff team, get mentored by people who do this every day, and earn a fully funded master's degree from Crown College along the way.

What you get

Three things at once.

1

Real ministry reps

Serve 28–30 hours a week on a CCC ministry team in your area of focus. Teach, lead, plan, recruit volunteers, and do the work you'll do for the rest of your career.

2

A master's, paid for

Earn your graduate degree online through Crown College while you serve. Tuition, books, and fees are covered.

3

A cohort and a mentor

Grow alongside a cohort of residents in weekly leadership development, and work with a mentor who knows your ministry area.

Who it's for

Built for the step after graduation.

This residency is for recent grads and early career adults who are exploring vocational ministry and want clarity, experience, and personal growth before choosing a long-term path.

  • You sense a call to ministry and want to test it in real life
  • You want a master's degree without the debt
  • You want people investing in you, not just a job description

No bachelor's degree yet? You can still apply. Residents without a college degree complete thirty credit hours of Bible and theology through Crown College.
