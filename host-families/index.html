Host or support a resident

Open your home to a future ministry leader.

A healthy, stable place to live is a big part of a resident's growth. Our host families give residents a private room, a safe home, and a front-row seat to everyday faith. Would you prayerfully consider hosting a resident?

Apply to host Support a resident

Why one to three years?

Instead of moving residents every few months, we ask hosts to commit to one to three years. Long-term stability is better for the resident's development, and it gives you time to build a real relationship.

What hosts do

What we ask of host families.

  • Pray and agree

    Listen to the Holy Spirit about whether this is right for your family. If you're married, you and your spouse should both be fully on board.

  • Provide a private space

    A private bedroom with standard furnishings, plus a private or semi-private bathroom. A basement, mother-in-law suite, or guest house works too.

  • Keep a clean, safe home

    Not spotless all the time. Generally clean and secure, so the resident feels comfortable and safe.

  • Host free of charge

    Don't expect payment for rent and utilities. It removes a major cost for the resident and helps the church financially.

  • Have reliable Wi-Fi

    Residents take online courses through Crown College, so a stable internet connection is required.

  • Communicate early

    Talk through house rules, boundaries, and expectations in the first few days.

  • Connect relationally

    Invite them to a meal or out for coffee. None of it is required, and both your boundaries and theirs are respected.

What residents commit to

  • Respect your personal space and house rules
  • Follow the same code of conduct as CCC staff
  • Clean up after themselves in private and shared spaces
  • Offer to help with light tasks like trash, vacuuming, or shoveling snow
  • Ask before inviting guests over
  • Provide their own groceries, meals, and transportation

How the residency team supports you

  • Sets expectations and boundaries with both host and resident
  • Provides mediation and conflict resolution when needed
  • Checks in quarterly and hosts two host family gatherings each year
  • Finds another home for the resident if the need ever arises

Next steps

How to become a host family.

  1. Read the host family guide

    It covers everything involved, including a communication guide to work through as a family.

    Download guide

  2. Apply online

    One member of the household fills out the host family application on Church Center.

    Apply on Church Center

  3. Complete a background check

    Every adult living in the home completes one. We ask the same of every incoming resident.

  4. Schedule a home visit

    A member of the residency team will walk through the space with you, in person or virtually.

Want to talk with a current or former host family first? Send us a note and we'll connect you.
