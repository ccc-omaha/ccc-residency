Frequently asked questions

How long is the residency?

Two years. Residents on the Master of Arts in Counseling with state licensure track serve three years.

Is the residency paid?

Residents raise their own monthly financial support, and CCC matches donations up to $400 a month per resident.

What does the master's degree cost?

Nothing. Your degree through Crown College is fully funded, including books and fees.

Is the degree online?

Yes. Coursework through Crown College is online, so you can complete it alongside your ministry work. Plan on 12–18 hours a week.

Where will I live?

You can live with a CCC host family in a private room, with no rent or utilities. Learn about host families.

Do I need a college degree?

No. Residents without a bachelor's degree complete the Crown 30 certificate, thirty credit hours of Bible and theology.

Can I choose my ministry area?

Yes. You'll choose a primary area of focus and work with a mentor in that area. See the areas of focus.

Do I need a car?

Yes. Residents provide their own vehicle and transportation.
