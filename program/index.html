The program

Two years of ministry, mentoring, and a master's degree.

Our purpose is to raise up and equip next-generation leaders for work in church ministry. CCC Church Residency combines focused leadership training and practical ministry experience with a graduate-level academic program, so you leave ready to serve your local church.

Your week

What a typical week looks like.

28–30hrs / Ministry
Hands-on work in your area of focus, plus training in the general skills every ministry leader needs.
12–18hrs / Graduate school
Online coursework through Crown College, on your own schedule.

Cohort leadership development with your fellow residents is built into the rhythm of the week.

What you'll develop

Ministry takes more than one skill.

The residency is built to give you a well-rounded set of competencies for full-time church ministry.

Leadership development

  • Creating and casting mission and vision
  • Strategic planning
  • Goal setting
  • Building and leading teams
  • Creating a reproducing, sending leadership culture

Ministry experience

  • Up-front communication like preaching and teaching
  • General pastoral duties like weddings and funerals
  • Pastoral counseling
  • Creating a volunteer culture: recruiting, training, and retaining
  • Evangelism
  • Specialized skills in your area of focus

Biblical and theological education

  • Graduate-level study through Crown College
  • Fully funded, including books and fees
  • Completed online alongside your ministry work

Areas of focus

Choose your ministry area.

Residents choose a primary area of focus and work with a mentor inside it, based on their calling, gifts, and passions.

  • Kids Ministry

    Help children and their families build a foundation of faith.

  • Middle School and High School

    Disciple students through the years that shape who they become.

  • College and Young Adults

    Reach and disciple college students and young adults across Omaha.

  • Adult Discipleship

    Help adults grow in their faith through groups, classes, and care.

  • Missions

    Mobilize the church to serve locally and around the world.

  • Worship Arts

    Lead worship and develop the musicians and teams behind it.

  • Communications and Creative

    Tell the church's story through design, video, writing, and digital media.

  • Tech and Production

    Run the audio, video, and lighting behind services and events.

  • Counseling and Care

    Walk with people toward healing. Pairs with the counseling degree tracks.

Not sure yet? That's fine. Tell us on your application.

Your degree

Pick your degree path.

Every degree is completed online through Crown College and is fully funded, including books and fees.

Two-year residency

  • Master of Christian Studies
  • Master of Ministry Leadership
  • Master of Arts in Pastoral Counseling (non-licensure)
  • Crown 30 certificate: thirty credit hours of Bible and theology, for residents without a bachelor's degree

Three-year residency

  • Master of Arts in Counseling with state licensure

Learn more about Crown College

Cost and support

How the money works.

Residents raise their own monthly support, and CCC matches it. Your degree is covered, and housing is available with a host family at no cost.

  • Raised support

    Friends, family, and churches partner with you through monthly giving.

  • Church match

    CCC matches the donations you raise, up to $400 a month.

  • Tuition

    Your Crown College degree, books, and fees are fully funded.

  • Housing

    Live with a CCC host family in a private room. No rent or utilities.

  • Transportation

    Residents provide their own vehicle and transportation.

Friends and family who want to partner with a resident can give online.
